## Quick rules before you hit send

- **Mirror the exact job title** from the posting in both the subject line and first paragraph.
- **Always change `[COMPANY]`** and include **one specific detail** about them — a generic
  letter reads as a mass send and gets skipped.
- **Send the matching résumé variant** (see the table above).
- **Keep it under ~250 words / 4 short paragraphs.** Recruiters skim.
- **Proofread the greeting and company name** — a wrong name is an instant reject.
- **Where to paste it:** for most PH remote applications, the email body or the
  application form's "cover letter" field is fine. Attach a PDF only if they ask.
- **Subject line for cold emails:** `Application for [JOB TITLE] — Joaquin Alec P. Hao`.
